## The client

TheTickSuit makes a one-piece barrier suit that protects people from ticks while they garden, walk in the woods, hike, or work outdoors. It sells directly to customers through its own website, [theticksuit.com](https://theticksuit.com).

## What we did

**Redesigned the website.** We rebuilt TheTickSuit's site to make it modern: a clear story about what the suit does, large outdoor photography, and a straightforward path from the homepage to the shop.

**A/B tested the UI for conversion.** Instead of guessing which layout would sell best, we tested versions of the interface against each other and kept the ones that converted best.

**Added a chat that answers customers 24/7.** Shoppers ask the same things before buying a suit: what size to order, how it fits, and how payment works. The chat answers those questions at any hour, which cut down the time the owner spent replying to emails.

**Built a review pipeline.** We set up a pipeline that asks customers for reviews, so the site shows more real social proof from people who have bought and used the suit.

**Manages their SEO and AI search discovery.** We handle TheTickSuit's search optimization and how the brand shows up when people look for answers in AI search tools, so the right customers can find it.

## Why it matters

A modern site gets attention, but the testing, the chat, the reviews and the search work are what turn visits into orders and take repetitive work off the owner's plate.
