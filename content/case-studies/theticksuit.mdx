## The client

TheTickSuit makes a one-piece barrier suit that protects people from ticks while they garden, walk in the woods, hike, or work outdoors. It sells directly to customers through its own website, [theticksuit.com](https://theticksuit.com), so the website is the storefront, the salesperson and the support desk all at once.

## The challenge

A product like this has to earn trust quickly. A shopper lands on the site, wants to know whether the suit really works, whether it will fit, and whether the brand is the real thing, and they want those answers before they spend money.

That put several jobs on one website:

- **Look credible.** The site had to feel modern and clear about what the suit does.
- **Convert.** Every visit that doesn't turn into an order is a missed sale, so the layout had to be good at moving people toward the shop.
- **Answer questions.** Shoppers kept asking about sizing, fit and payment, and every one of those questions meant an email that someone had to write back.
- **Show proof.** New customers look for reviews from people who have actually bought and used the suit.
- **Be found.** People search for tick protection, and increasingly ask AI tools, so the brand needed to show up in both.

## Our approach

We treated the website as a system instead of a design project. First we rebuilt it so the foundation was modern and clear. Then we layered on the things that turn visitors into customers: testing the layout for conversion, a chat that answers questions around the clock, a pipeline that collects reviews, and ongoing work on search and AI discovery.

## What changed

- **The site looks modern and tells a clear story.** The homepage now leads with what the suit does and moves shoppers toward the shop.
- **Design decisions come from testing.** The interface was A/B tested for conversion, so the layouts that stayed are the ones that performed best.
- **Customers get answers at any hour.** The chat handles questions about size, fit and payment, and the team spends less time replying to emails.
- **The site shows more social proof.** The review pipeline brings in real feedback from customers who have bought and used the suit.
- **Search and AI discovery are looked after.** We manage both, so the work continues after launch.

## Still ongoing

This isn't a project that finished and went quiet. We still manage TheTickSuit's SEO and AI search discovery, and the testing and the chat keep improving as we learn what shoppers actually ask and respond to.
