## The client

MassimiLaw is a New York City civil rights and employment law firm. It represents individuals harmed by unlawful workplace practices, police misconduct, and government abuse, from discrimination and wrongful termination to excessive force and false arrest.

## The challenge

The firm had no website. For a law firm that matters more than it might seem, because of who is looking.

People who need a civil rights or employment lawyer are usually in a hard moment: they've lost a job, been mistreated at work, or had an encounter with the police. They search on their phone, often late at night, and they want three questions answered fast:

- **Does this firm handle my kind of problem?**
- **What happens if I reach out?**
- **How do I reach someone, right now?**

With no website, none of those answers existed online.

## Our approach

We built the site around those three questions and nothing else. Every section exists to answer one of them, in plain language, with no legal jargon standing between a worried person and the next step.

## What we built

- **A clear front page.** A plain-spoken headline, a phone number and a "Call Now" button that stay visible at the top of every page, and a free consultation button right under the intro.
- **A page for every practice area.** 16 dedicated pages: ten for employment law (such as discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination and wage and hour violations) and six for civil rights (such as police brutality, excessive force and false arrest), reachable from the menu and from a grid on the front page. A line for anyone whose situation isn't listed invites them to get in touch.
- **One obvious next step.** A free case evaluation section with a click-to-call button, the firm's hours, a short list of what the consultation covers, and an answer to "what happens when you call?"
- **A step-by-step process.** Four steps, from the free consultation to resolution, so people know what working with the firm looks like before they call.
- **Straight answers.** An FAQ for the questions potential clients ask most.

## What changed

The firm went from no online presence to a website of its own. Anyone can now read what the firm handles, see how the process works, and call for a free consultation, and the firm has a place to point people to when they're asked for a website.
